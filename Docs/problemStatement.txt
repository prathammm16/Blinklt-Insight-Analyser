# Blinkit Insight AI – Problem Statement

## Overview

Blinkit has become a part of millions of users' weekly shopping routines by providing ultra-fast delivery of groceries, snacks, beverages, and household essentials. As customers continue using the platform, their purchasing behavior becomes increasingly repetitive. Most users repeatedly purchase products from the same categories and rarely explore new categories, even though Blinkit offers a wide variety of products across grocery, personal care, baby care, pet supplies, electronics, kitchen essentials, and more.

This repetitive shopping behavior limits category expansion, reduces cross-selling opportunities, and impacts long-term customer lifetime value.

The Growth Product team aims to increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers (MAC) who purchase from at least one new category every month. Achieving this requires understanding why customers hesitate to explore unfamiliar categories and identifying opportunities to make product discovery more personalized, relevant, and trustworthy.

To support this objective, we are building an AI-powered Product Discovery and Review Intelligence system named **Blinkit Insight AI**.

---

# Objective

Build an AI-powered Discovery Engine that automatically analyzes customer feedback from multiple public sources to uncover recurring user pain points, shopping behaviors, unmet needs, and opportunities related to cross-category discovery.

Instead of manually reading hundreds or thousands of customer reviews, Product Managers should receive structured, evidence-backed insights that can directly support product decisions and growth initiatives.

---

# Business Goal

Increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers who purchase products from at least one new category every month.

Examples:

- Grocery customers purchasing Personal Care products.
- Snack buyers discovering Beverages.
- Household essentials buyers exploring Baby Care.
- Grocery users discovering Pet Supplies.
- Existing customers expanding into Kitchen Essentials.

---

# Product Goal

Develop an AI-powered research assistant capable of automatically analyzing customer conversations at scale and answering strategic product questions such as:

- Why do users repeatedly purchase from the same categories?
- What prevents users from exploring new categories?
- How do users currently discover products?
- What role do habits play in shopping behavior?
- Which user segments are more likely to experiment?
- What information do customers need before trying a new category?
- Which frustrations appear repeatedly across multiple platforms?
- What opportunities exist to improve category discovery?

---

# Data Sources

The Discovery Engine will analyze publicly available customer feedback from multiple sources, including:

- Google Play Store Reviews
- Apple App Store Reviews
- Reddit Discussions
- Community Forums
- Social Media Conversations (where available)
- Product Review Platforms

The system should be flexible enough to support additional data sources in future versions.

---

# System Responsibilities

Blinkit Insight AI should automatically perform the following tasks:

### Data Collection

- Collect customer reviews from supported sources.
- Support manual CSV upload for MVP.
- Future support for direct API integrations.

---

### Data Cleaning

- Remove duplicate reviews.
- Filter spam and irrelevant content.
- Normalize review text.
- Handle multilingual reviews where applicable.

---

### Sentiment Analysis

Classify customer reviews into:

- Positive
- Neutral
- Negative

Generate overall sentiment distribution.

---

### Theme Detection

Automatically identify recurring discussion topics such as:

- Shopping habits
- Product discovery
- Recommendation quality
- Delivery experience
- Pricing concerns
- Trust issues
- Product availability
- Search experience

The system should not rely on predefined categories but instead discover themes dynamically using AI.

---

### Theme Clustering

Group similar reviews together to identify recurring patterns instead of treating every review individually.

Example:

Reviews mentioning:

- "Recommendations are irrelevant"
- "Suggestions don't match my needs"
- "I ignore recommendations"

should be grouped under a common theme such as:

**Poor Recommendation Quality**

---

### Shopping Behaviour Detection

Identify common customer behaviors including:

- Repeat purchasing
- Mission-driven shopping
- Search-first behavior
- Low browsing activity
- Category loyalty
- Price-sensitive shopping

---

### Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD)

Extract the underlying customer job behind shopping behavior.

Example:

"When I open Blinkit, I want to complete my weekly grocery shopping as quickly as possible without wasting time exploring products."

---

### Root Cause Analysis

Identify why observed behaviors exist.

Examples:

- Customers prioritize speed over exploration.
- Lack of trust in unknown brands.
- Information overload.
- Poor personalization.
- Limited contextual recommendations.

---

### Opportunity Identification

Based on discovered insights, identify product opportunities that could increase category discovery.

Examples:

- AI-powered personalized recommendations
- Mission-aware suggestions
- Smart shopping bundles
- Lifestyle-based recommendations
- Educational product cards
- Trust-building recommendation explanations

---

### PM Insight Generation

Generate structured product insights supported by real customer evidence.

Each insight must include:

- Insight title
- Supporting reviews
- Business impact
- User impact
- Confidence level

The system should never generate unsupported conclusions.

---

# Output

The Discovery Engine should generate a Product Management research report containing:

- Dataset Summary
- Source Distribution
- Sentiment Analysis
- Top Themes
- Theme Frequency
- User Pain Points
- Shopping Behaviors
- Jobs-To-Be-Done
- Root Causes
- Opportunity Areas
- Product Recommendations
- Executive Summary

---

# Success Criteria

The AI Discovery Engine will be considered successful if it can:

- Analyze hundreds or thousands of customer reviews.
- Reduce manual research effort.
- Identify recurring themes with high accuracy.
- Generate actionable product insights.
- Support Growth Product Managers in identifying opportunities to increase cross-category purchases.

---

# Non-Goals

The MVP will not:

- Generate personalized recommendations for individual customers.
- Modify Blinkit's production systems.
- Replace primary user research.
- Make autonomous product decisions.
- Predict customer behavior using historical transaction data.

Instead, it serves as a research intelligence system for Product Managers.
